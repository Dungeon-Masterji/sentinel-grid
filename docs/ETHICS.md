# Ethics note: synthetic workforce data

Every worker in Sentinel Grid's occupational pillar is synthetic. The 15 sites are real
UAE locations taken from OpenStreetMap, but the roster, shifts, absences and PPE readings
attached to them are generated by `packages/pipelines/synth/workforce.py` from a fixed
random seed. The nationality, role, age and sex mixes are modelled on public aggregates
(ILO migrant-worker estimates, embassy-reported diaspora sizes, UAE labour-force survey
sector shares and MoHRE midday-break rules; see `configs/workforce_mix.yaml`). Where no
published breakdown exists, as with nationality by sector, the weights are stated
assumptions, not observed statistics. Workers carry opaque ids only, with no names or
identifiers of real people, and site worker counts are not operator figures. The data
exists to test whether environmental stress shows up in workforce-level signals. It
**must not be used for individual employment decisions**: not hiring, firing, rostering,
pay, discipline, insurance or immigration status. It must not be used to profile, rank
or predict the behaviour of any nationality, age group or sex either. Alerts derived from
it describe site conditions (heat, air quality, crew-level absence) and point to
site-level controls such as rest breaks, shade, hydration and shift timing. They never
